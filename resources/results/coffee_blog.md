# Claim Audit: coffee_blog

**Evidence score:** 66.2/100

This audit reveals that while basic data regarding weight loss and sleep in a small study is supported, broader claims about the universal efficacy and health benefits of intermittent fasting are unsupported or overstated. The research suffers from significant methodological weaknesses, including a small sample size, selection bias from social media recruitment, and a lack of baseline health tracking. Consequently, the findings are unreliable for making generalized health conclusions.

## 4. 🟠 overstated  `comparative`

> Intermittent fasting melts fat faster than any other method.

**Evidence (verified verbatim):** "A groundbreaking study of 30 volunteers at a single gym found that people who skipped breakfast for four weeks lost an average of 2 kilograms."

**Why:** The study only compared 30 people at one gym and did not compare the results against other weight loss methods, making the claim of superiority unsupported.

**Issues:** small_sample, selection_bias, missing_baseline

**Defensible rewrite:** A small study of 30 gym-goers found that skipping breakfast for four weeks was associated with an average weight loss of 2 kilograms.

*confidence 1.00*
